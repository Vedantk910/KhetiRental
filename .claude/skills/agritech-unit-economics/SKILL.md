# Agritech Unit Economics & Pricing Model Protocol
## 1. Dynamic Depreciation & Pricing Formula
$$\text{Hourly Rate} = (B_{\text{base}} \times \mu_{\text{season}} \times \delta_{\text{soil}}) + (D_{\text{wear}} \times \kappa_{\text{age}}) + (F_{\text{consumption}} \times P_{\text{fuel}})$$
- $B_{\text{base}}$: Baseline rate per hour by horsepower tier (35 HP, 45 HP, 55 HP, 75 HP 4WD).
- $\mu_{\text{season}}$: Seasonal surge multiplier ($1.0$ to $1.45$ during 12-day sowing/harvest compression).
- $\delta_{\text{soil}}$: Soil resistance coefficient ($1.0$ alluvial loam, $1.25$ black-cotton clay).
- $D_{\text{wear}}$: Depreciation wear factor ($\text{Acquisition Cost} / 10,000\text{ hours}$).
- $\kappa_{\text{age}}$: Vintage multiplier ($1.0 + 0.05 \times \text{Age in Years}$).
- $F \times P$: District diesel price pass-through.
